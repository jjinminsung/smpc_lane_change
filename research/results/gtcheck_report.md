## B. Reference point of GT positions

### B1. Kinematic side slip (GT only, 53 09-* bags)

For a reference point x m ahead of the zero-slip point (rear axle at low lateral acceleration), heading - course = -atan(x * kappa). After removing the constant heading bias gamma (median on straights), x_ref = -(beta - gamma)/kappa over curve samples (abs kappa > 0.008 1/m). Rear axle -> ~0 (drifting negative with speed as tyre slip moves the zero-slip point forward); bbox centre -> +(L/2 - rear overhang) ~ +1.2..1.5 m for cars.

**Conclusion B.** Rear face (ahead) and front face (behind) are both shifted forward by the same ~1.2-1.4 m, independent of range, ego speed and whether the ego is stopped. A centre reference would give ~0 on both sides. Together with B1, **/Object_topic npc.position is the vehicle's rear axle** (cars: ~1.25 m behind the bbox centre, i.e. ~L/2-1.25 m ahead of the rear bumper; 5-7 m vans/trucks ~1.2 m; 7-13 m buses ~3.2-3.7 m). **/Ego_topic position = /odom = ego rear axle** (B1, and the LiDAR offset matches the rear-axle offset expected from published Prius/K5 wheelbase and overhangs, ~1.35 m, within ~0.15 m, so lidar_x = 1.0 is consistent). LiDAR alone measures c - (mount error); B1 removes that ambiguity.

Impact on object mode (node assumes centre): target_rear front edge is ~1.25 m too far back, so the rear bumper gap is overestimated by ~1.25 m (unsafe side); front vehicles' rear edge is ~1.25 m too close (conservative). In the LC windows of the 3 replays, 95 of 1974 target_rear frames had a published rear bumper gap in [9, 15) m and 19 of them would fall below the 9 m base gap after the correction; 30 of 2111 target_front frames below the 14 m front gap would clear it.

The longitudinal error (+1..2.5 m behind, -0.5..-2 m ahead) is the near-face bias of partial boxes plus the GT rear-axle reference (B).

## Bugs found (object_topic path, beyond the offset) and suggested fixes (not applied)

1. **Reference point (B, safety-relevant).** `objectCallback` uses npc.position as the bbox centre, but it is the rear axle. Fix: shift along the heading before building the footprint, e.g. `obj.x += c*cos(yaw); obj.y += c*sin(yaw)` with a new `object_topic_ref_to_center_m` (1.25 m for cars), ideally a per-`npc.name` table or a length rule (>= 7 m: ~3.2 m).
2. **v_lat is vehicle-frame.** `velocity_in_map=false` copies velocity.y (side slip, ~0). Build map vx/vy from velocity.x/y and the heading (minus the 1.365 deg bias) and set `velocity_in_map=true`, so `makeVehicle` projects onto the lane tangent. Latent today (cut-in v_lat branch and lateral modes are disabled in smpc_decision.yaml).
3. **Heading bias +1.365 deg.** Subtract it from npc.heading (param, or compute from MGeo/UTM convergence). Negligible in object mode; the same bias in /imu -> tracker map transform is a real LiDAR-path bug (fix in lidar_ttc_tracker or kcity_localizer: yaw - 1.365 deg).
4. **Ego/object time alignment.** The latest /odom is up to 0.06-0.1 s older than the /Object_topic stamp (ego_s error up to 1.7 m, overlap flips). In object mode use /Ego_topic (same stamp as /Object_topic, + offset) or extrapolate /odom to the frame stamp.
5. **Rate / bursts.** Subscriber queue 1 drops 24-34% of frames that arrive in bursts. Harmless for freshness; if a regular rate matters, process the newest frame on a fixed 20 Hz timer, and cut the ~9 ms callback with a windowed (segment-index hint) projection instead of brute force.
6. **Lane coverage.** NPCs outside the 4 CSVs are given lane 1/3 via the 3.4 m nearby clearance gate; consider requiring abs(d) <= ~2.5 m (or adding the lane-0 continuation / a lane left of lane 3 as non-target lanes) so they cannot be read as target-lane traffic.
